THE PROBLEM

Heat is more than a temperature.

Extreme heat can affect health, outdoor activity and everyday life. The challenge is not only knowing that it is hot — it is understanding what that means for you.

01

Information is scattered

Weather, warnings, maps and safety guidance are often found in different places.

02

Risk isn't always obvious

The same temperature can affect different people in different ways.

03

Action matters

People need clear and understandable guidance, not just raw weather data.

OUR MISSION

Make heat safety simple.

HeatShield brings important heat information together in one accessible platform. Instead of overwhelming people with complex information, we focus on understandable risk levels, timely alerts, visual maps and practical safety guidance.
