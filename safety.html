EVERYDAY PROTECTION

Small actions. Real protection.

+

Stay hydrated

Drink water regularly instead of waiting until you feel thirsty.

◐

Find shade

Reduce direct sun exposure, especially during peak heat.

+

Dress lightly

Choose loose, lightweight clothing whenever possible.

!

Reduce activity

Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest hours.

WHO NEEDS EXTRA CARE

Heat doesn't affect everyone equally.

Some people are more vulnerable to heat-related illness and may need additional support.

01

Older adults

Check on older family members, neighbours and people living alone.

02

Young children

Keep children cool, hydrated and away from prolonged direct sunlight.

03

Outdoor workers

Take frequent cooling breaks and avoid unnecessary exposure.

04

People with health risks

Follow medical advice and take extra precautions during heat events.
