RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

What you should do now

Simple actions can reduce heat exposure and help protect you and the people around you.

01

Stay hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day, even when you do not feel thirsty.

02

Find a cooler place

Reduce heat exposure by moving indoors or finding a shaded, cooler environment.

03

Reduce sun exposure

Use shade and suitable clothing when you need to be outdoors.

04

Check on others

Look out for children, older adults and people who may be more vulnerable to heat.

IMPORTANT

Know the signs of heat illness.

Severe weakness, confusion, fainting or other serious symptoms require immediate attention.
