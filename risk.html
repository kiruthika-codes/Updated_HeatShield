WHAT TO DO NEXT

Recommended actions

01 ◒

Reduce direct exposure

Prefer shade or a cooler indoor place, especially during the hottest part of the day.

02 ♧

Hydrate and take breaks

Drink water regularly and pause physical activity if you feel overheated.

HOW THIS SCORE WORKS

Know the limits

The prototype classifies the reported feels-like temperature using these thresholds:

LowBelow 35°C
Moderate35–39.9°C
High40–44.9°C
Extreme45°C and above

This is an educational prototype, not a substitute for official weather alerts or medical advice. Conditions can change quickly.
